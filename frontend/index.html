06 · Why INK exists

Every drawer has a part nobody needs anymore.

Somewhere in a hostel room there is a sensor from a project that ended last semester. Somewhere else, a first-year is stuck waiting for the exact same part, and buying a new one would eat the whole month's budget.

INK connects those two people. Seniors pass on what they've finished with, juniors build what they dreamed of.

Less waste.

Working parts stay in use instead of drawers.

Lower cost.

Built by students.
